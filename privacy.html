Personal Performance Lab

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Purpose and scope

Personal Performance Lab is a private, single-user wellness and fitness analytics application.

Information collected

With the owner's authorization, the application retrieves available Oura measurements, including sleep, activity, readiness, heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen and temperature data. It may also store voluntarily entered or imported workout, nutrition and lifestyle records and authorization credentials.

Use of information

Information is used for personal tracking, visualization, statistical analysis and wellness insights. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and access

Application data and credentials are stored on the owner's computer. Remote access is intended to use a private authenticated network. Authorized integrations communicate with their respective providers to retrieve data. The owner controls any exported reports or backups.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls retention and may delete local records, credentials, backups and exports, and revoke Oura authorization through the Oura account. Deleting local data does not delete records held by Oura or other providers.

Public website

This informational website contains no personal biometric data and does not request Oura credentials or use analytics scripts. GitHub Pages, its hosting provider, may process technical access information under its own privacy statement.

Medical disclaimer

The application is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions.

Contact

Privacy inquiries may be submitted via the project repository. Do not include health information or credentials in public issues.
